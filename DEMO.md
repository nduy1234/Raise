# 60-second demo

Cast: **Dana** (founder, owner), **Bo** (co-founder), **Sam** (advisor), all in one iMessage thread with Raise.

| t | who | text | Raise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Dana | hi | Asks for the startup name and one-liner |
| 0:05 | Dana | Ledgerly — AI bookkeeping for Shopify sellers. Fintech, pre-seed, raising $1.5M, SF, $8k MRR +20% MoM | Saves the profile and sends a 3-line recap: "Say *find investors*" |
| 0:12 | Dana | Bo is my co-founder, Sam is our advisor | Records both relationships |
| 0:15 | Sam | find investors | Researches, then posts a ranked top 10. Each line: fit reason, a recent deal, source link. "24 verified." |
| 0:30 | Dana | draft for i1 | Posts a 100-word intro citing i1's recent fintech deal, then "Owner or co-founder: *approve i1*" |
| 0:40 | Sam | approve i1 | "Only the owner or a co-founder can approve." (refused) |
| 0:45 | Bo | approve i1 | "Approved by Bo (co-founder). Here's the final text to send from your inbox. I never send." |
| 0:52 | Dana | sent i1 | Marks i1 contacted |
| 0:55 | Dana | status | `i1 contacted … | researched 23 | approved 0 | contacted 1` |

Talking points: every investor claim has a link, approval is role-gated from
the Plow roster, and nothing is auto-sent.
